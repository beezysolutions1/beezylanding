Before We Start

What is Google Business Profile?

Think of your Google Business Profile as a free digital signboard for your business on Google.

A properly optimized profile can help potential customers discover your business when they are actively searching for the products or services you provide.

PRACTICAL EXAMPLE

A business owner in Kochi searches: "Best digital marketing agency in Kochi"

If Beezy Solutions appears, the customer can see:
  • 📍 Location & Directions
  • ⭐ Real Google Reviews & Star Rating
  • 📸 Real Photos & Past Work
  • 📋 Services Catalogue & Pricing
  • 📞 Direct One-Tap Call Button

Master Checklist

14-Point Google Business Profile Audit

Check each item before launching your profile optimization:

✓ 1. Business Information & NAP is 100% consistent across web
✓ 2. Most accurate Primary Category chosen
✓ 3. Comprehensive list of specific Services added
✓ 4. Service areas match actual radius served
✓ 5. Business description includes natural keywords without stuffing
✓ 6. Direct phone number is verified and working
✓ 7. Working website link with SSL
✓ 8. Business hours and holiday schedules updated
✓ 9. High-resolution photos uploaded regularly (Office, Team, Work)
✓ 10. Google Posts published weekly with Call-To-Action buttons
✓ 11. NFC Google Review Card or direct review link deployed
✓ 12. Review request WhatsApp script automated after every sale
✓ 13. All reviews responded to with personalized, keyword-rich replies
✓ 14. Enquiry button and booking link tested end-to-end
